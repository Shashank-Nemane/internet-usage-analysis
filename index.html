NetPulse
Student Internet Analytics Platform
The Idea
University students spend hours online every day — but how, how much, and how consistently?

NetPulse analyzes daily internet usage across four key behavioural dimensions — Education, Entertainment, Social Media, and Communication — using seven rigorous statistical lenses: Mean, Median, Mode, Range, Standard Deviation, Coefficient of Variation, and Skewness.

The platform features a high-performance C++ REST Backend (Port 8080) paired with a resilient, reactive frontend. Uncover behavioral trends, evaluate individual routines against peer averages and ideal productivity benchmarks, and receive a live 0–100 Digital Wellness Score with an actionable plan.
